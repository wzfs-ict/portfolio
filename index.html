Why Computer Science?

My career began in technology, but my calling grew in education.

My career began in professional IT and technology infrastructure before moving into education. Teaching technology showed me that students need more than the ability to use digital tools — they need to understand systems, solve problems, write programs and think computationally.

That has led me to increasingly focus my teaching and curriculum interests on Computing and Computer Science. I want students to move beyond being users of technology and become thoughtful, capable creators who understand how digital systems work.

My Computer Science Vision

Students should not encounter Computer Science for the first time in senior high school.

A strong Computer Science pathway progressively develops computational thinking, problem solving and programming from the early years through secondary education.

Students should move from understanding digital systems, to creating algorithms, to writing programs, to designing increasingly complex computational solutions.

The goal is not simply to prepare students for an examination. It is to help students think, create, debug, communicate and solve problems like computer scientists.
